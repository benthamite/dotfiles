#+title: Archived agent material
#+author: Pablo Stafforini

This directory retains retired Claude and Codex programmatic skills together
with the lists, digests, and result files needed to understand their former
operation. The two archived trees are kept as historical counterparts.

Archived =SKILL.md= files are not installed into the live Claude or Codex skill
roots and are not discoverable as active skills. The generated
[[file:../agents/skill-inventory.org::*Archived][skill inventory]] lists them under =Archived= so their presence is not confused
with activation.

The retained outputs are records, not a queue of work to resume. Move material
out of =archive/= only as an explicit reactivation, with its current trigger,
dependencies, safety rules, paired-tool status, and tests reviewed as a new
change. Ordinary maintenance should leave the history intact.
