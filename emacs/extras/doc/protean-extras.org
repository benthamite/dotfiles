This manual describes the features and customization options for the Emacs Lisp file =protean-extras.el=.

* Overview
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:overview
:END:

Protean's inbox shows, beside the line at point, the task above and the message it is about below (Protean's manual, "The preview"). Protean names no client: it asks the functions of ~protean-message-open-functions~ to open the message. =protean-extras.el= provides one such function per channel I read in Emacs, each of which prepares its client's buffer at the message and hands it to Protean without displaying anything itself. With the buffer each gives where the message is in it, so that the preview marks it: the Slack message's =ts=, the Signal message's =sgn-message-rowid=, telega's button of the message. An email is its buffer alone and needs no mark.

| Channel  | Function                         | Client                                   |
|----------+----------------------------------+------------------------------------------|
| email    | ~protean-extras-open-email~      | mu4e's renderer, in a buffer of its own   |
| Slack    | ~protean-extras-open-slack~      | emacs-slack, the room or the thread      |
| Signal   | ~protean-extras-open-signal~     | sgn (~sgn-message-buffer~)               |
| Telegram | ~protean-extras-open-telegram~   | telega                                   |

WhatsApp has none yet: Protean shows its own view of those messages, saying so, until wsp can open a message.

~protean-extras-openers~ lists the four, for ~protean-message-open-functions~.

* Email
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:email
:END:

#+findex: protean-extras-open-email
~protean-extras-open-email~ finds the message by its Message-ID with =mu find=, and renders its file in =*Protean mail*= with mu4e's own renderer, in ~mu4e-view-mode~, so that it reads as in mu4e. mu4e's view and headers buffers are left as they are, and the message's flags are not changed. A Message-ID mu does not know is an error, which Protean shows as the reason for its own view.

* Slack
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:slack
:END:

#+findex: protean-extras-open-slack
#+vindex: protean-extras-slack-teams
~protean-extras-open-slack~ opens the message's room at it, or, for a reply in a thread, the thread, and hands over the buffer once the message is loaded. The team is the one ~protean-extras-slack-teams~ names for the message's Protean account, an alist of (ACCOUNT . TEAM-NAME); it must be registered and connected. An account with no team, a team not connected and a room emacs-slack does not know are errors.

* Signal and Telegram
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: h:signal-telegram
:END:

#+findex: protean-extras-open-signal
#+findex: protean-extras-open-telegram
~protean-extras-open-signal~ hands over sgn's chat buffer at the message (~sgn-message-buffer~). ~protean-extras-open-telegram~ hands over telega's chat buffer once telega has gone to the message; Telegram must be running.
